Register your photographs against the camera that took them

Genesis learns your camera's sensor fingerprint from RAW files already on your machine, then lets you register photographs under your own signature on a public, on-chain record. The fingerprint and the RAW archive never leave your computer -- only hashes and commitments do. Get the desktop app below.

How it works

  1. Enrol your camera, once

    Point the app at ~40 RAW files already on your machine. It computes your sensor's noise fingerprint locally and commits to it on Base -- the fingerprint itself never leaves your computer.

  2. Register a photograph

    The app hashes the image and scores it against your fingerprint, then records the registration under your camera's on-chain identity.

  3. Anyone can check a copy later

    A stripped, resized or re-posted copy can still be traced back to the original registration from the public verify page -- no account, no login.

Unsigned builds for now: macOS will ask you to right-click and choose "Open" the first time; Windows will show a SmartScreen prompt -- choose "More info" then "Run anyway". Code signing is on the roadmap, not yet done.
