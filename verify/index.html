Verify an image

Drop a photograph. We tell you whether its owner registered it — or that we have no record.

We certify origin, not truth: body X’s owner registered this image at time T. A fingerprint score on its own proves nothing — it can be planted — so only an owner’s registration counts. We do not say “authentic”, “AI-free”, or “verified real”, and the absence of a record means nothing.
