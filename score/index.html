Score a photo against your fingerprint

Runs entirely on this device. Nothing here is uploaded, sent to a server, or logged anywhere -- not the fingerprint file, not the photo.

This is your own enrolled sensor fingerprint (K), the file the desktop app wrote when you enrolled your camera. It is never uploaded anywhere -- it stays in this browser tab's memory for this session only, and is used solely to compute the score below, entirely on this device.

A JPEG, PNG, or TIFF at (close to) the camera's native resolution.

PCE (Peak-to-Correlation-Energy) measures how strongly a photo's noise correlates with your camera's sensor fingerprint. Genesis's measured decision threshold is PCE_THRESHOLD = 100.0 (fingerprint/prnu.py) -- comfortably above the null scores seen against unrelated bodies, comfortably below genuine matches, but resting on limited evidence (see docs/gates.md). A PCE on its own is not a verdict you can show someone else: it is forgeable, and only an on-chain registration (the Verify page) is.
